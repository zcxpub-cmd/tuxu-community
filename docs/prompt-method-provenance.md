# Design-method provenance for Community v0.2

This repository's protocols, category guidance, validation code and short recommendation reasons were written specifically for Tuxu Community. No third-party full prompt, reference library, asset, model setting or example image was copied into this release. Local plugin configuration, credentials and customer material were outside this review.

The review was limited to installed skill descriptions, selected general design sections of their `SKILL.md` files, and available licence notices. The table records the installed versions examined; it does not claim their upstream contents or licence status remain unchanged.

| Installed reference | Version examined | Review and reuse boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `zy-cinematic-realism` | 1.2.0 | Its wrapper identifies CC BY-NC 4.0. The full method and prompts were excluded from implementation and redistribution; its presence is recorded for transparency. |
| `cinema-dna-21x9x3` | 1.2.2 | Selected high-level discussion of scene plausibility and visual intention was reviewed. A redistribution licence was not established in this limited review. No prompt wording, sequence format or reference material was reused. |
| `fantasy-dongfang-jianyuehaibao` | 2.0.0 | Selected discussion of information hierarchy and independent reference interpretation was reviewed. A redistribution licence was not established. No layout, title treatment, template or prompt was copied. |

| `video-shotcraft` | 1.0.0 | The installed source includes an Apache-2.0 licence. General discussion of visual coherence and scene purpose was reviewed. No source code, shot card, asset or template was copied. The package links to its [public library](https://vincentwei1021.github.io/video-shotcraft/library.html). |

The independently written Community approach is deliberately simple: identify what the illustration needs to explain; retain only supplied facts; make the subject and its relationships readable; choose coherent light and material treatment; and keep exact editorial text outside generated pixels. The fourteen categories describe different ways of presenting those facts. They do not import a plugin's proprietary strategy, promise a named camera's output, or reproduce an identifiable reference composition.

These general design ideas informed a new public protocol rather than a translated or shortened third-party prompt. In particular, unknown or restrictive licences were not treated as permission to republish content. This document identifies references without exposing installation paths or local account names.
